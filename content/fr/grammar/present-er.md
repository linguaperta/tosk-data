# The present of -er verbs: je parle

Most French verbs end in **-er** (*parler*, *aimer*, *travailler*). Drop *-er* and add the endings:

| | parler | aimer |
|---|---|---|
| je | parle | aime (*j'aime*) |
| tu | parles | aimes |
| il / elle / on | parle | aime |
| nous | parlons | aimons |
| vous | parlez | aimez |
| ils / elles | parlent | aiment |

- *-e, -es, -e, -ent* are silent: *je parle, tu parles, ils parlent* sound the same.
- The French present covers English "I speak", "I am speaking" and "I do speak": *Je travaille.* = I work / I'm working.
- With *depuis* it says how long something has been going on: *J'habite ici depuis deux ans.* (I have lived here for two years.)
- Spelling: *-ger* verbs keep an *e* before *-ons* (*nous mangeons*); *-cer* verbs take *ç* (*nous commençons*).
- Some -er verbs change their stem: *acheter → j'achète*, *appeler → j'appelle*, *préférer → je préfère* (not before *-ons, -ez*).
