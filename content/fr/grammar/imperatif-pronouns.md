# Donne-moi, lève-toi: the imperative with pronouns

In an **affirmative** imperative the pronouns go **after** the verb, joined by hyphens, and *me / te* become **moi / toi**:

| | affirmative | negative |
|---|---|---|
| direct | *Regarde-le !* | *Ne le regarde pas !* |
| indirect | *Parle-lui !* | *Ne lui parle pas !* |
| reflexive | *Lève-toi ! Asseyez-vous !* | *Ne te lève pas ! Ne vous asseyez pas !* |
| me | *Écoute-moi !* | *Ne m'écoute pas !* |
| two pronouns | *Donne-le-moi !* | *Ne me le donne pas !* |

- In the negative everything is back in the normal order, before the verb.
- *y* and *en* bring back the *-s* of -er verbs: *Penses-y ! Achètes-en !*
- Useful: *Dépêche-toi !* (Hurry up!) *Tais-toi !* (Be quiet!) *Calmez-vous !* (Calm down!) *Excusez-moi.* (Excuse me.)
