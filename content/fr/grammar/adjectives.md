# Adjectives: petit, petite, petits

Adjectives agree with their noun in **gender** and **number**: add **-e** for the feminine and **-s** for the plural.

| | singular | plural |
|---|---|---|
| masculine | petit | petits |
| feminine | petite | petites |

- Ending in *-e* already: no change for the feminine (*facile, triste, rouge*).
- Common patterns: *heureux → heureuse*, *actif → active*, *cher → chère*, *bon → bonne*, *ancien → ancienne*, *gentil → gentille*. Masculine plurals: *-x* stays (*heureux*), *-al → -aux* (*national → nationaux*).
- Irregular: *beau, belle, beaux, belles* · *nouveau, nouvelle* · *vieux, vieille* · *blanc, blanche* · *long, longue* · *faux, fausse*. Before a vowel: *un bel homme, un nouvel ami, un vieil arbre*.
- **Position**: most adjectives come **after** the noun (*une voiture rouge*, *un livre intéressant*). Short common ones come **before**: *beau, joli, bon, mauvais, grand, petit, gros, jeune, vieux, nouveau, premier* (*un petit chat, une jolie maison*).
- Some change meaning with position: *un grand homme* (a great man) / *un homme grand* (a tall man); *mon ancien patron* (former) / *une maison ancienne* (old).
