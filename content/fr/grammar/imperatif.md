# The imperative: parle ! finissons !

The imperative gives orders, advice and instructions. It has three forms, without a subject pronoun:

| | parler | finir | attendre |
|---|---|---|---|
| (tu) | parle | finis | attends |
| (nous) | parlons | finissons | attendons |
| (vous) | parlez | finissez | attendez |

- The forms are those of the present, but **-er verbs drop the -s of the tu-form**: *Tu parles → Parle ! Tu manges → Mange !* (also *Va !* from *aller*).
- The *-s* comes back before *y* and *en*: *Vas-y ! Manges-en !*
- *nous* means "let's": *Allons-y ! Partons !*
- **Negative**: *ne ... pas* around the verb: *Ne parle pas si fort ! Ne mangez pas trop !*
- Polite requests often use *vous* or a question: *Fermez la porte, s'il vous plaît. Pouvez-vous fermer la porte ?*
