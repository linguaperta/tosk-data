# Ce, cette, celui-ci: demonstratives

**Ce** (this / that) agrees with its noun:

| masculine | before a vowel | feminine | plural |
|---|---|---|---|
| ce livre | cet ami | cette maison | ces livres |

- To contrast "this" and "that", add *-ci* and *-là*: *ce livre-ci* (this book), *ce livre-là* (that book).
- **Pronouns** (this one, that one, the one): *celui* (m.), *celle* (f.), *ceux* (m. pl.), *celles* (f. pl.): *Quelle robe ? Celle-ci ou celle-là ?*
- They are followed by *-ci / -là*, *de*, or a relative pronoun: *celui de Paul* (Paul's), *celle qui parle* (the one who is speaking), *ceux que j'aime*.
- *ceci* (this) and *cela / ça* (that) refer to things or ideas without a gender: *Cela ne m'étonne pas. Ça va ?*
