# The future: je parlerai

The futur simple says what **will** happen. Add the endings to the **infinitive** (drop the final *-e* of *-re* verbs):

| | parler | finir | vendre |
|---|---|---|---|
| je | parlerai | finirai | vendrai |
| tu | parleras | finiras | vendras |
| il / elle | parlera | finira | vendra |
| nous | parlerons | finirons | vendrons |
| vous | parlerez | finirez | vendrez |
| ils / elles | parleront | finiront | vendront |

- The endings are those of *avoir* in the present: *-ai, -as, -a, -ons, -ez, -ont*.
- The near future (*je vais partir*) is more common in speech; the futur simple sounds more definite or more formal: *Je t'appellerai demain.*
- After *quand, lorsque, dès que* French uses the future where English uses the present: *Quand tu arriveras, on mangera.* (When you arrive ...)
- After *si* (if) use the present, never the future: *S'il pleut, je resterai à la maison.*
