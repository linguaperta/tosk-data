# Reading the passé simple: il parla

The passé simple is the past tense of **literature, tales and history**. In speech it is replaced by the passé composé, so you only need to **recognise** it.

| | parler | finir | vendre |
|---|---|---|---|
| je | parlai | finis | vendis |
| tu | parlas | finis | vendis |
| il / elle | parla | finit | vendit |
| nous | parlâmes | finîmes | vendîmes |
| vous | parlâtes | finîtes | vendîtes |
| ils / elles | parlèrent | finirent | vendirent |

- You will mostly meet the **il / ils forms**: *Il entra, regarda autour de lui et sortit.* (He came in, looked around and left.)
- *-er* verbs: *-a, -èrent*; others mostly *-it, -irent*.
- Don't confuse *il parla* (passé simple) with *il parlera* (future) and *il parlait* (imparfait).
- Like the passé composé it tells events; the imparfait still describes the background: *Il pleuvait quand il arriva.*
