# Je vais partir, je viens d'arriver

Two everyday constructions with an infinitive:

| | construction | example |
|---|---|---|
| near future | aller + infinitive | *Je vais partir.* (I'm going to leave.) |
| recent past | venir de + infinitive | *Je viens d'arriver.* (I've just arrived.) |

- *aller* is conjugated, the main verb stays in the infinitive: *Nous allons manger. Ils vont voir un film.*
- The near future is the usual future in speech, especially for plans and things about to happen: *Attention, tu vas tomber !*
- *venir de* is only used in the present and the imparfait: *Il venait de partir quand tu as appelé.* (He had just left.)
- Pronouns go before the infinitive: *Je vais le faire. Il vient de me téléphoner.*
