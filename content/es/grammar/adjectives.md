# Adjectives: bueno, buena, buenos

Adjectives agree with their noun in **gender** and **number**, and usually come **after** it: *un coche rojo*, *una casa blanca*.

| | masculine | feminine |
|---|---|---|
| -o / -a | nuevo, nuevos | nueva, nuevas |
| -e | grande, grandes | grande, grandes |
| consonant | fácil, fáciles | fácil, fáciles |

- *El chico alto*, *la chica alta*, *los chicos altos*, *las chicas altas*.
- Adjectives in **-e** or a consonant have one form for both genders: *un examen difícil, una pregunta difícil*.
- A few short adjectives often go before the noun: *buen*, *mal*, *gran*. *Bueno* and *malo* lose their *-o* before a masculine singular noun: *un buen día*, *un mal momento*. *Grande* becomes *gran* before any singular noun and then means "great": *una gran ciudad*.
- With *ser* and *estar* the adjective agrees too: *Las sillas son nuevas.* *Ella está cansada.*
- Comparison: *más ... que* (more ... than), *menos ... que* (less ... than). Irregular: *mejor* (better), *peor* (worse).
