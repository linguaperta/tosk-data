# Ser: to be (what and who)

Spanish has two verbs for "to be". **Ser** says *what* or *who* something is: identity, origin, profession, character, time and dates. It is irregular, so learn it by heart.

| | ser |
|---|---|
| yo | soy |
| tú | eres |
| él / ella / usted | es |
| nosotros | somos |
| vosotros | sois |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | son |

- Subject pronouns are usually left out: *Soy estudiante.* = I am a student. The verb ending already shows the person.
- *Usted* (polite "you") takes the él/ella form: *¿Usted es de Madrid?* *Ustedes* takes the ellos form: *¿Ustedes son hermanos?*
- *Vosotros* (you, plural) is used in Spain. In Latin America people say *ustedes* to friends too: *Ustedes son muy amables.*
- No article before a profession: *Es médico.* (He is a doctor.)
- Origin and material: *Somos de Holanda.* *La mesa es de madera.*
- Time: *Son las tres.* (It is three o'clock.) *Es la una.*
- *Es* (is) and *eres* look alike; *es* is "he/she/it is", *eres* is "you are".
