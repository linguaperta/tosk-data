# Present tense of -are verbs

Most Italian verbs end in **-are**. Remove *-are* to get the stem, then add the ending for the person.

| | parlare (to speak) | mangiare (to eat) | cercare (to look for) |
|---|---|---|---|
| io | parl**o** | mangi**o** | cerc**o** |
| tu | parl**i** | mang**i** | cerch**i** |
| lui/lei | parl**a** | mangi**a** | cerc**a** |
| noi | parl**iamo** | mang**iamo** | cerch**iamo** |
| voi | parl**ate** | mangi**ate** | cerc**ate** |
| loro | parl**ano** | mangi**ano** | cerc**ano** |

- Endings: **-o, -i, -a, -iamo, -ate, -ano**.
- Verbs in **-care / -gare** add an *h* before *i* to keep the hard sound: *cerchi, cerchiamo, paghi, paghiamo*.
- Verbs in **-iare** do not double the *i*: *mangi* (not *mangii*), *studiamo*.
- The *loro* form is stressed like the *io* form: *PARlano*, *ABitano*.
- One present tense covers "I speak", "I am speaking" and "I do speak": *Parlo italiano.*
- Subject pronouns are usually dropped; the ending already shows the person.
