# Present tense of -ire verbs

Verbs in **-ire** come in two groups. Both use the same *noi* and *voi* endings, but the second group inserts **-isc-** in the other four persons.

| | dormire (to sleep) | capire (to understand) |
|---|---|---|
| io | dorm**o** | cap**isco** |
| tu | dorm**i** | cap**isci** |
| lui/lei | dorm**e** | cap**isce** |
| noi | dorm**iamo** | cap**iamo** |
| voi | dorm**ite** | cap**ite** |
| loro | dorm**ono** | cap**iscono** |

- Like *dormire*: *partire, sentire, aprire, offrire, seguire, servire, scoprire, soffrire*.
- Like *capire*: *finire, preferire, pulire, costruire, spedire, sparire, suggerire*.
- The infinitive does not tell you the group: learn each verb with its *io* form (*dormo*, *capisco*).
- *-isco* and *-iscono* are pronounced with a hard *sk*; *-isci* and *-isce* with a soft *sh*.
